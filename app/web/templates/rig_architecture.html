Run order

Cash first, so we never chase money we have

  1. Cash Application runs before anything else. In 2025 Paidly chased a long-standing customer who had already paid by wire, because the money took days to be matched. A receipt that plausibly pays an invoice is now a hard block on chasing it.
  2. Account Context reads what support knows (finance can't see Zendesk): disputes hiding in "solved" tickets, cancellations, legal threats, stale contacts.
  3. Controller turns that into holds with a reason and a route: the AM, Priya or Dana.
  4. Collections drafts only what is left, in Ledgerline's own voice, with no threatening wording. Nothing is sent without Priya.
